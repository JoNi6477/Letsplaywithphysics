Meet the founder

Akshay Kumar

Akshay is a physics faculty member at an IIT-JEE coaching institute in Hyderabad, with a Master's degree in Physics from the University of Hyderabad (Central University). He teaches NEET and JEE aspirants with a Socratic, inquiry-first approach — encouraging students to question, derive and discover rather than simply memorise.

Let's Play With Physics (letsplaywithphysics.in) is his independent physics education platform, created to bridge the gap between rote exam preparation and genuine conceptual understanding. It serves students preparing for NEET and JEE Mains across India with free and premium resources.

Our mission

Physics is a way of thinking.

Physics is not a collection of formulas to memorise. Our mission is to make every student comfortable with asking “why?” and following the answer wherever it leads. We believe the best exam preparation and the deepest conceptual understanding are the same thing — not opposites.
