About This Platform

Let's Play With Physics (letsplaywithphysics.in) is an independent physics education resource created to bridge the gap between rote exam preparation and genuine conceptual understanding.

The platform serves students preparing for NEET and JEE Mains/Advanced examinations across India, providing free and premium resources including formula sheets, practice question banks, and timed mock tests.

Our Mission

Physics is not a collection of formulas to memorise — it is a way of thinking. Our mission is to make every student comfortable with asking "why?" and following the answer wherever it leads. We believe the best exam preparation and the deepest conceptual understanding are the same thing — not opposites.
