Please read the instructions carefully

  1. The countdown timer at the top right shows how much time you have left. When it reaches zero, the test ends and is submitted automatically.
  2. Each correct answer gets +4 marks, each wrong answer −1 mark. Unanswered questions get 0.
  3. The Question Palette on the right shows the status of every question:
    1 You have not visited the question yet.
    2 You have not answered the question.
    3 You have answered the question.
    4 You have NOT answered the question, but have marked it for review.
    5 You have answered the question and marked it for review. This answer will be evaluated.
  4. Selecting an option does not save it. Click SAVE & NEXT to save your answer and go to the next question.
  5. SAVE & MARK FOR REVIEW saves your answer and marks the question so you can look at it again.
  6. MARK FOR REVIEW & NEXT marks the question for review without saving a new answer.
  7. CLEAR removes the answer you chose for the current question.
  8. Clicking BACK, NEXT or a question number in the palette moves you without saving the current selection.
