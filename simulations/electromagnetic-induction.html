Lenz's law, step by step
ε = − N dΦ/dt
Faraday: an emf is induced only while the flux through the coil is changing. Faster motion, more turns or a stronger magnet give a larger emf.
Lenz: the induced current opposes the change that causes it (the minus sign). This follows from conservation of energy.
Try: hold the magnet still inside the coil. The flux is large but the emf is zero.
Model: the magnet is treated as a point dipole on the coil axis; self-inductance is ignored.
