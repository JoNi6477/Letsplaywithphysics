Welcome to Let's Play With Physics (letsplaywithphysics.in). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use and protect your information when you visit our website. By using this site, you agree to the terms described below.

1 Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Usage Data: Pages visited, time spent, browser type, device type, and IP address — collected automatically via analytics tools.
  • Student Account Data: If you create an account, we store your name, email address, optional phone number, the plans you have bought, their start and expiry dates, and the amount paid. Your password is handled securely by our login provider and is never visible to us.
  • Voluntarily Provided Data: If you contact us via WhatsApp or email, we receive the information you share (name, message, phone number).
  • Mock Test Data: When you submit a mock test, your answers, score and time taken are saved to your student account so you can review your results and track your progress.

2 How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected to:

  • Improve the content and user experience of the website
  • Create and manage your student account and give you access to the content you have paid for
  • Respond to queries or messages sent by you
  • Analyse site traffic and usage patterns
  • Display relevant advertisements through Google AdSense

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

3 Cookies & Tracking

Our website uses cookies to enhance your browsing experience. Cookies are small text files stored on your device. We use:

  • Analytics Cookies: To understand how visitors interact with our site (e.g., Google Analytics).
  • Advertising Cookies: Google AdSense uses cookies to serve personalised ads based on your interests and prior visits.

You can disable cookies through your browser settings at any time. Note that some features of the site may not function correctly without cookies.

4 Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services that may collect data independently:

  • Google AdSense – Displays advertisements. Google may use cookies to personalise ads. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Analytics – Tracks site usage. Data is anonymised and aggregated.
  • Supabase – Stores student accounts and access records on servers in Mumbai, India. See Supabase's Privacy Policy.
  • FormSubmit – Delivers enquiry and "notify me" forms to our email inbox.
  • WhatsApp – If you contact us via WhatsApp, your message is governed by WhatsApp's privacy policy.

We have no control over these third-party privacy practices and encourage you to review their respective policies.

5 Children's Privacy

This website is intended for students preparing for NEET and JEE examinations, who are typically 16 years of age and above. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly. Students under 18 may create an account only with the permission of a parent or guardian, which they confirm when signing up. A parent or guardian can ask us to view or delete their child's account at any time by contacting us.

6 Data Security

We take reasonable precautions to protect your information. Our site is served over HTTPS, which encrypts data in transit. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7 Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to any personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your personal data
  • Opt out of personalised advertising via Google Ad Settings

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details below.

8 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised date. We encourage you to review this page periodically. Continued use of the website after changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
